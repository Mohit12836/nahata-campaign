**Service ID:** `IN-MP-PHARM-001`  
**Status:** NEEDS_REVIEW; partial verified government service listing, not final production SOP

**Category:** Regulated commercial/professional service

## Officially supported observations
- Official MP Pharmacy Council notice says fresh applicants need Samagra, Sthaniya Niwasi (local-resident proof) and DigiLocker.
- During profile creation the applicant's name, father's name, DOB, address and photograph are fetched from Samagra and cannot be edited in that form.
- The notice describes a paperless process, without physical document submission/office visit, and removal of earlier slot booking.

## Safe operator research workflow
1. Confirm applicant's required Samagra, local-resident proof and DigiLocker access.
2. Verify mandatory professional education/qualification requirements against current Pharmacy Council rules; not established by portal notice alone.
3. Applicant authenticates required official records and reviews imported details; do not override locked identity attributes.
4. Stop on eKYC/identity verification. Complete only after Council confirms registration.
