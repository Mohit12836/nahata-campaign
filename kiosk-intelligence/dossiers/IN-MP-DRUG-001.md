# Madhya Pradesh — New Drug Retail / Wholesale Sales Licence

**Service ID:** `IN-MP-DRUG-001`  
**Status:** NEEDS_REVIEW; partial verified government service listing, not final production SOP  
**Source:** [SRC-023](https://services.india.gov.in/service/detail/apply-for-new-drug-retail-whole-sales-license-madhya-pradesh)  
**Checked:** 2026-10-26  
**Category:** Regulated commercial/professional service

## Officially supported observations
- National Government Services Portal confirms MP new retail / wholesale drug sales licence application through MPOnline and MPOnline kiosks.
- This is a regulated establishment licence; it is not the same as personal pharmacist registration.

## Safe operator research workflow
1. Identify retail, wholesale or combined licence and licensed premises details.
2. Verify required registered pharmacist/competent person, ownership, premises, technical documents and drug category on current MP FDA official checklist; these details are NOT yet fully verified.
3. Use official MPOnline/FDA entry and operator authorization.
4. Verify payment, inspection/authority checkpoints and final licence issuance; application acknowledgement is not issued licence.
