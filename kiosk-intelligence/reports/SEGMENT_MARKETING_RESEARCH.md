## Testable local hypotheses — do not present as measured results
1. Deadline-dependent applications may drive high-volume short bursts near closing dates.
2. Complex regulated business licences may allow higher skilled-service value, but costs, authorization and net margins are unknown.
3. Repeat low-complexity services could build footfall, but the per-transaction margin may be modest.
4. Engagement through lawful, opt-in coaching institutes, professional and agricultural trade associations may outperform untargeted broadcasts; run small consenting tests and record conversions.

Do not advertise an official deadline without a same-period source check, imply government endorsement, promise issuance or charge a prohibited registration fee. Record actual margin as **customer permitted fee/commission minus payment cost, employee time cost, partner cost and rework**.
