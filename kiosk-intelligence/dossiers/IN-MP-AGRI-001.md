## Officially supported observations
- National Government Services Portal confirms MP fertilizer sales licensing via enabled state, eDistrict/CSC/MP kiosk channels.
- MP CM Helpline guidance describes separate fertilizer authorization renewal and category-specific official renewal amounts; do not apply those renewal fees to new application.

## Safe operator research workflow
1. Identify whether retailer or wholesaler and district/state scope.
2. Review current MP agriculture eDistrict conditions, authorization, qualification and required documents.
3. Confirm exact licence/renewal action, fee and authorised kiosk channel.
4. Applicant/operator completes document verification and payment with consent.
5. Track department decision and deliver verified authority-issued licence.
